ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Etah Derick Manyo

Founder, Massif One

AI & Digital Business Strategist

Etah Derick Manyo is a Cameroonian digital professional, creative technologist, and entrepreneur passionate about using technology and artificial intelligence to solve practical business problems.

With experience spanning graphic design, social media management, digital communication, project coordination, website management, and AI-powered content creation, Derick has worked with businesses and organizations to improve their digital presence, create engaging visual content, and streamline everyday operations.

His growing expertise in artificial intelligence has led him to explore practical applications of AI in business — including AI-powered customer service, content creation, automation, and digital business management.

As the founder of Massif One, Derick is building a new generation of business technology designed with African SMEs in mind. His vision is to make powerful digital tools and AI accessible to businesses that may not have large teams, expensive software, or dedicated technology departments.

Massif One was born from a simple belief: small businesses deserve the same intelligent technology that large companies use to compete and grow.

Through Massif One, Derick aims to create an AI-powered operating system that helps businesses manage customers, sales, inventory, orders, communication, and daily operations from one platform — while giving entrepreneurs an intelligent AI business employee that can work alongside them 24/7.

His long-term vision is to help build an African technology ecosystem where AI is not simply a futuristic concept, but a practical tool that empowers entrepreneurs, creates opportunities, increases productivity, and helps businesses grow.

His mission is simple: build technology that makes African businesses stronger, smarter, and more competitive.
